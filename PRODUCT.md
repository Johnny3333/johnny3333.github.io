# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, prospective PhD supervisors, and academic collaborators reviewing Zhaoqi Song's research and academic background.

## Product Purpose

A static academic portfolio that explains research interests, presents project evidence and manuscripts, and provides a downloadable CV.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, understated. Preserve the existing Luka identity and its serif/sans-serif pairing.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic commercial landing pages, decorative dashboards, inflated scientific claims, and effects that compete with research figures.

## Design Principles

- Make the research question and contribution clear before implementation details.
- Lead with scAnyOmics and its Any-to-Any completion capability.
- Keep manuscript evidence easy to search and verify.
- Use research figures as the main visual material.
- Preserve a simple, buildless GitHub Pages website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast and text, responsive navigation, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and clear image descriptions.
