About Me

Hello! I am Zhaoqi Song, currently an M.Eng. student in Bioengineering at Xiamen University. I am advised by Prof. Chaoyong Yang and expect to graduate in June 2027. Before joining Xiamen University, I received my bachelor's degree in Bioinformatics from Fujian Medical University in June 2024.

My work sits at the intersection of computational biology, bioinformatics, deep learning, and spatial transcriptomics. My current focus is on foundation models for virtual cells, particularly Any-to-Any completion of single-cell multi-omics profiles, alongside multimodal methods for spatial omics and biomedical discovery.

Research Interests

Foundation Model

Transferring knowledge from pretrained biological models to unified multi-omics representations.

Virtual Cell

Modeling cellular identity, perturbation responses, and biological ageing from incomplete molecular profiles.

Spatial Omics

Integrating tissue images and molecular measurements to resolve spatial organization at cellular scale.

AI for Bioinformatics

Developing interpretable, reproducible AI methods to connect complex biological data with biological questions.
